# Joint client handover: 24 October 2026

## Change

The installer submits to Verisko staff. Technical approval leaves the commercial job Installed and awaiting client handover. Only a site supervisor named on that installation can meet the client and save the result. Global Operations/Admin access is insufficient without assignment; an assigned Team Lead can complete this step without pricing or crew-pay access.

The acceptance form reuses the existing client name and care references, asks five final checks, captures the client’s drawn signature or typed full name, requires client acceptance and the signed-in supervisor’s confirmation. The server stamps the actor and time. All commands use the same installation and linked quote/contact. It requires a connection, protects competing saves and retries, and blocks old job-form bypasses.

Fix needed requires a problem and next action, sets the installation Needs correction and the commercial job In progress. Installer must record a new completed repair session, note and new photo before resubmitting. Technical review must pass again before another client meeting. Client unavailable keeps the job awaiting handover, with reason/next action and no invented signature. Signed handovers are immutable. Historical records and commercial terms are preserved.

Signatures and review history are visible only to assigned site supervisors. Other authorized installation readers see the result and next step; Sales sees workflow progress. The source commercial job holds signature-recorded metadata, not strokes. No new footage/GPS access, payment, pricing or warranty policy.
