# Ready for site visit

Open **Contacts → Site visit queue**.

Sales selects a saved contact and can add a short note. Team Lead and Operations see the request in **Unassigned**. Sending a request does not book a visit or earn visit pay.

- **I'll take this:** become the owner.
- **Assign to someone:** choose a Team Lead or Operations person. Once assigned, only the owner or Admin can pass the lead on.
- **Send back:** add a reason. The lead goes back to its previous owner. If there is no previous office owner, it returns to the original salesperson, who can send it again.
- Use **With me** for your work and **All requests** to see the shared queue.

Sales sees the owner and ownership history for their contacts. The original salesperson stays attached to the contact. Other salespeople cannot see it. Installers cannot open this queue. Team Lead sees submitted requests, not other salespeople's unsent contacts.

Ownership changes need a connection. If two people take the same lead, only one claim succeeds. Refresh to see the owner. Existing handovers to Operations appear in the queue too.

## Visit and quote progress

The current owner (Team Lead or Operations), or Admin, sees one next-step button:

1. **Confirm visit:** choose the agreed date and time in Uganda. The same contact moves to Prospects and one linked visit appears in Site visits. No second contact is created.
2. **Mark visit done:** record the date the team visited and assessed the site. This feeds the existing UGX 15,000 visit-pay rule, once per eligible customer. A confirmed booking alone does not earn visit pay.
3. **Record quote sent:** record the date an existing quote was sent. An optional quote number is visible to the office only. This button does not create a priced quote or send a message.

Sales sees all three steps and their dates in the queue. Sales cannot edit them. The history records who saved each step and when. Quote status does not create payment commission.

Queue visits are managed here. Their fields are restored from the authoritative queue record when older devices save the main workspace, so they cannot be silently undone. Duplicate new visits for the same queue contact are rejected. A new owner can continue the existing progress after an office handover. After confirmation, pass the job to another office person instead of returning it to prospecting.

Job payments and later installation steps continue through their existing screens.
